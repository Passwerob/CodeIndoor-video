# Scene Planning Preferences and Follow-Up Modification Requirements

Overall scene-scale preference (explicitly requested by the user): when the user does not constrain size, by default make the usable space of the whole room/scene somewhat larger and more spacious. After accommodating the required furniture and necessary functional zones, comfortable circulation, operation and breathing-room margins should still remain; the minimum size that can fit everything is not the planning goal, and functional zones must not be shrunk to squeeze things in. When the user gives an explicit size, that limit is obeyed. This preference is to be implemented in later system modifications.

## 2026-11-03: Default to More Spacious, Avoiding Cascading Rework Caused by Tight Space

Source: the user explicitly asked for this to be recorded after the living-room test and the solvability diagnosis; this entry is a record only, and no system code, model prompt or original scene was modified.

User's judgment and direction: a planned room that is small relative to the furniture set leads to crowding, and further amplifies conflicts among spacing, orientation, usage zones, circulation and layout repair. Later modifications should make the planning Agent lean more toward spacious space.

Later implementation should follow:
- When the user does not specify a room size, prefer a scale that comfortably accommodates the required furniture and daily activity, proactively reserving circulation, seat entry/exit and usage margins; do not take "the furniture just fits" as the goal.
- First account for the overall capacity of the furniture set, usage zones and routes, then freeze room size and layout. When space is tight, allow going back to the Agent's own choices of space size, furniture specifications, quantity or grouping to adjust, instead of only repeatedly shifting coordinates.

- When the user gives an explicit size, respect that limit and do not enlarge it on your own; adjust the non-essential choices inferred by the Agent within that range, and state clearly which requirements truly cannot be satisfied together.
- A rug is a floor layer that furniture projections may cover, and should not be required to be fully separated from furniture like an independent solid footprint. Keep the necessary checks such as ground contact, boundary and real circulation.

Diagnostic boundary: tight space is an amplifying factor to be handled first, but not the sole cause of all failures this time; the already-recorded issues such as inconsistent local direction semantics and revisions overwritten by the old contract still need handling. Do not mask constraint contradictions by enlarging the room without limit.

## 2026-11-03: Reserve Functional Space by Actual Activity, Distinguishing Zone Types and Overlap Rules

Source: the user further proposed that planning should also reserve the actual usage space of components such as doors and furniture, and consider wall-adjacent vs. non-wall-adjacent placement and whether a rug can be walked on or stepped over. This section is a later design requirement and has not been implemented.

- A component declares, according to its actual purpose, its solid footprint, the human operation/entry-exit zone and the motion zone of movable parts, and connects the usage zones that must be reached to the circulation network. Passive decor or a rug is not forced to get a separate human operation zone; a ring of exclusive clearance must not be mechanically added to every part.
- An ordinary single-leaf swing door is by default planned to open normally to 90°, not by default required to open to 180°. Let A be the effective width from the hinge axis to the far edge of the leaf; in the door's local plane coordinates, preferably reserve an approximately A×A square region covering the whole motion from closed to 90°; the square is placed according to the doorway position and orientation, and accounts for leaf thickness, handle and necessary margin. This is a functional reservation zone, not the shape of the door leaf itself. By default this region avoids solids that would block the door opening, ensuring normal use in a simple, conservative way, without introducing complex sector planning each time just to save a corner.
- Only when the user explicitly requires it or a specific door mechanism needs opening to 180° is the corresponding motion range handled; the local-plane swept bounding box of an ideal thin leaf rotating continuously 180° from the closed position is about a 2A×A rectangle. 2A×A must not be used as the default reservation standard for all ordinary room doors; double-leaf doors, sliding doors and so on should be computed separately according to their own mechanisms.
- The true planar sweep of a 90° leaf is approximately a quarter-circle sector, and A×A is its conservative rectangular bound. When complex geometry, or corners overcovered only by the bounding box, cause a case to be judged unsolvable, the swept shape may be used for an exact re-check; until re-checked, keep the default square clearance, and do not hand the true motion zone over to an obstruction.
- The standing and passing space at the doorway is expressed separately from the leaf sweep zone. Check the motion process from closed to the target open state, and reachability in the required open state; checking only the position where the door is already open and parked beside the frame is not enough to prove the door can open normally.
- Cabinet doors, drawers, chairs and so on are considered separately for opening, pulling out, entering/exiting or pulling back; the zone shape, direction, size and height come from the actual structure and activity, and are not uniformly set as one rectangle in front of the whole piece of furniture.
- Functional zones use the component's local coordinates and update with size, position and orientation; the against-wall/away-from-wall strategy is decided jointly with the accessible faces. Furniture that may have its back against the wall does not additionally require a passage behind it; furniture that needs motion or operation at the rear must still have space. Against the wall does not mean permission to pass through the wall.
- Handle solid collision, motion obstruction, human use and circulation separately. Functional zones do not all mutually exclude: compatible circulation and brief operation space may be shared; for mutually exclusive actions needed at the same moment, verify against the required usage scenario. A door-leaf motion zone may overlap some circulation space, but the opening operation and the necessary circulation states must be achievable.
- Ground-hugging, walkable floor layers such as rugs may overlap furniture projections, human operation zones and circulation zones, and are not excluded like tall solids. Height, thickness and the specific physical conditions are still checked: walkable does not mean one can also pass when the gap under a door is insufficient; ordinary furniture must not be treated as a traversable surface merely because it could theoretically be stepped over.
- Zone checks should consider vertical extent, expected contact and the specific object pair, to avoid judging a collision merely because XY bounding boxes intersect. Combinations with a clear interaction relationship, such as tables and chairs, or doors and door frames, need motion and usage judgment at the combination level.
- Space capacity is evaluated by the geometric union of solids and necessary functional zones, sharing rules and circulation connectivity, and the area of each bounding box must not be directly added up; at the same time keep the spacious margin required in the previous section.

Check of current implementation: src/scene_spatial.py already has AccessArea, local front/back/left/right and rectangular doorway approach; src/layout_solver.py already has usage-zone clearance and the floor_covering exemption; the existing door-leaf checks are mainly door-edge parking after opening and doorway clearance. This requirement is to supplement motion sweep, activity semantics and compatibility rules on top of the existing mechanisms, not to build a new unified exclusive-rectangle scheme from scratch.
